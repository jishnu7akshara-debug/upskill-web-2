The Aster method

Four movements, repeated until mastery.

Every module follows the same rhythm, so you always know what's expected — and what good looks like.

I.

Learn from practitioners

Concept sessions led by engineers who use these tools every day — recorded, annotated and searchable.

II.

Build in studios

Small studios of 24 work on weekly briefs in pairs and squads, the way real product teams operate.

III.

Defend your work

Critiques and code reviews every Friday. You explain your decisions — and learn to take feedback well.

IV.

Graduate into roles

Demo day with hiring partners, interview coaching and referrals until you sign an offer.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Do I need prior coding experience?

No. Programs start from fundamentals and the aptitude conversation helps us place you on the right track and pace.

Can working professionals join?

Yes — weekend and evening studios are designed for people with full-time jobs.

Is placement guaranteed?

We don't promise guarantees. We provide unlimited interview preparation and referrals to hiring partners until you're placed, subject to program completion.

What does a typical week look like?

Around 20 hours: concept sessions, studio build time, one critique and one 1:1 mentor check-in.

Are scholarships available?

Merit and need-based scholarships of up to 40% are awarded after the aptitude conversation.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes, plus a verified project portfolio and a mentor recommendation letter on completion.
